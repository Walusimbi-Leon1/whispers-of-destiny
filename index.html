Whispers of Destiny

A Love Novel

By Walusimbi Leon (SGSS)

About

Whispers of Destiny is a sweeping romantic novel that asks one timeless question: What if the person you were meant to be with was sitting just a few seats away? A story about fate, courage, and the whispers that guide us toward the people we are meant to find.
